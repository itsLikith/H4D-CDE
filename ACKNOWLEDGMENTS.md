> **AI-Augmented Hexagonal Voxelization for Scalable Conflict Detection in Urban Air Mobility**

**Authors**  
Dr. Deepudev Sahadevan¹, Dr. Hannah Al Ali¹, and Dr. Chilka Mahesh²

¹ Faculty of Mathematics and Data Science, Emirates Aviation University, Dubai, UAE  
² CNS Department, Airports Authority of India, Kalaburagi Airport, India

**Conference**  
2025 8th International Conference on Signal Processing and Information Security (ICSPIS)
